Configure your personal user profile, notification preferences, and workspace security settings under `/dashboard/settings`.

---

## 1. Profile Management

* **Display Name**: Update your official user display name visible across team comments and activity logs.
* **Avatar Image**: Upload a professional user avatar.
* **Email Address**: Update your registered account email.

---

## 2. Notification Preferences

Control how and when PMG Tracker 360 alerts you:
* **Tender Deadline Alerts**: Toggle email notifications 48 hours and 24 hours prior to bid submission closing dates.
* **Team Invitations**: Receive alerts when you are added to a new organization workspace.
* **Project Status Changes**: Stay informed when a tender is awarded or a milestone status is updated.

---

## 3. Transferring Organization Ownership

Organization **Owners** can securely transfer primary ownership of a workspace to another active team member:
1. Go to `/dashboard/settings` under the **Organization Ownership** section.
2. Select the target recipient from the eligible team members dropdown list.
3. Click **Initiate Transfer**.
4. Confirm the transfer prompt. Upon completion, the recipient becomes the new **Owner**, and your role transitions to **Admin**.
