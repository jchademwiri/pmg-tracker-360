This guide covers your personal account settings: your profile, your notifications, and (for workspace Owners) handing the workspace to someone else.

---

## 1. Your profile

1. Click **Settings** in the left menu, then open the **Profile** tab.
2. You can update:
   - **Your name** — shown to your team.
   - **Your photo** — upload a profile picture.
   - **Your email address** — your sign-in email.

---

## 2. Email notifications

Choose what you want to hear about:

- **Tender deadline alerts** — get an email **48 hours** and **24 hours** before a closing date.
- **Team invitations** — get notified when you're added to a new workspace.
- **Project updates** — get notified when a tender is awarded or a project status changes.

Turn each one on or off under **Settings → Notifications**.

---

## 3. Transfer workspace ownership

If you're the **Owner** and you want someone else to take over the workspace:

1. Click **Settings** in the left menu.
2. Find the **Organization Ownership** section.
3. Choose the team member you want to make the new **Owner**.
4. Click **Initiate Transfer** and confirm.

Once confirmed, the recipient becomes the new **Owner**, and your role changes to **Admin**.
