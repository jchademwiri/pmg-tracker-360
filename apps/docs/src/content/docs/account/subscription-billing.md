PMG Tracker 360 offers different plans depending on the size of your business. This guide explains how plans work and how to manage yours.

---

## 1. Choosing a plan

Plans differ in a few areas:

| What changes | Free | Paid plans |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Workspaces you own** | 1 | More (see your plan) |
| **Team members** | Limited | More seats |
| **File storage** | Limited | More space |
| **Support** | Community | Email / priority support |

> [!TIP]
> The exact limits for your plan are always shown in the app under **Billing** — use that as the source of truth.

---

## 2. Check your plan & limits

1. Click **Billing** in the left menu.
2. You'll see your **current plan** and how much of each limit you're using (for example, *Workspaces: 1 of 1* or *Storage: 1.2 GB used*).

---

## 3. Upgrade

1. Open **Billing** in the left menu.
2. Click **Upgrade Plan** on the plan you want.
3. Follow the checkout steps to confirm.

Your new limits apply right away.

---

## 4. Downgrade or cancel

- Before downgrading, check that you're not over the lower plan's limits (for example, more than one owned workspace on the Free plan).
- If you are, you'll be asked to close or remove extra workspaces before the downgrade can complete.
