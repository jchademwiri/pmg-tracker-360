**PMG Tracker 360** offers flexible subscription plans tailored to small contractors, growing businesses, and enterprise teams.

---

## 1. Plan Comparison Matrix

---

## 2. Managing & Upgrading Your Plan

To view your active subscription or upgrade:
1. Navigate to `/dashboard/billing`.
2. Review your active **Resource Usage Indicators** (e.g. *Owned Organizations: 1 / 1*, *Storage: 1.2 GB / 5 GB*).
3. Click **Upgrade Plan** on the target plan card (e.g., *Starter* or *Pro*).
4. Complete the simulated checkout process (or payment checkout). Your workspace limits will immediately expand.

---

## 3. Downgrading or Cancelling Subscriptions

* If you downgrade to a lower tier (e.g., from *Pro* to *Free*), ensure your active owned organization count does not exceed the destination plan limit (1 workspace cap).
* If you exceed the workspace cap, the system will prompt you to archive or delete extra workspaces before the downgrade can proceed.
