Quick answers to the questions we hear most from new users. If you don't find what you need, reach out via the **Support** menu in the app or the **Contact** page on the website.

## Accounts & Sign-In

### Do I have to verify my email?
Yes. After signing up you'll receive a verification email — click the link before you can sign in. If it hasn't arrived, check your spam folder or request a new one from the sign-in page.

### Can I sign in without a password?
Yes, two ways:

- **Google sign-in** — use the "Continue with Google" button.
- **Magic link / passcode** — choose the magic-link option on the sign-in page and you'll receive a sign-in link and a 6-digit passcode by email.

### I forgot my password. What do I do?
Use the **Forgot Password** link on the sign-in page. We'll email you a secure reset link.

## Workspaces & Teams

### What is an organization workspace?
An organization workspace is your company's private area in PMG Tracker 360. All clients, tenders, projects, purchase orders, and team members belong to a workspace — data is never shared between workspaces.

### Can I belong to more than one organization?
Yes. If you're invited to another organization's workspace, it appears in the **Workspace Switcher** in the sidebar and you can move between workspaces freely.

### How do team members join my workspace?
Organization **Owners** and **Admins** invite team members by email from the **Organization** page. Invitees accept the invitation from their email (they must have a verified account), and you can assign their role (`Admin`, `Manager`, or `Member`) when inviting.

### How many organizations can I own?
The **Free** plan allows **one** owned workspace; the **Pro** plan allows **two**. You can still join additional workspaces as a member via invitation on any plan.

### Can I transfer ownership of my workspace?
Yes — Owners can transfer primary ownership to another member under **Settings → Organization Ownership**. The recipient must be a member with a verified email.

## Tenders, Projects & Purchase Orders

### What do the tender statuses mean?

| Status | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Open** | Bid is being compiled; documents and pricing are being prepared. |
| **Evaluation** | Bid submitted to the buyer and under review. |
| **Awarded** | Contract awarded — ready to convert into a project. |
| **Lost** | Bid was not awarded to your organization. |
| **Closed / Cancelled** | Deadline passed or the opportunity was cancelled. |

### How do I turn a won tender into a project?
Open the awarded tender and click **Create Project from Tender**. Project details (name, client, and contract value) are pre-filled from the tender.

### Are tender numbers and PO numbers unique?
Tender numbers must be unique **within your organization** (two organizations may track the same tender). Purchase order numbers are unique **across the entire platform** — a PO number can never be reused by any organization.

### Can I export my reports?
Yes — the Reports module supports **PDF and Excel** exports for tender registers, submission trends, and per-client summaries.

## Billing & Limits

### What are the plan limits?
See [Subscription & Billing](/account/subscription-billing/) for the full comparison of owned workspaces, team seats, and storage across plans.

### Can I delete my workspace?
Only the **Owner** can delete a workspace, and it requires typing the workspace's name to confirm. Deletion permanently archives the workspace's tenders, projects, purchase orders, and memberships.

## Troubleshooting

### I can't switch to another organization.
Use the **Workspace Switcher** at the top of the sidebar and select the workspace you want. If an organization doesn't appear there, you either haven't accepted its invitation yet or you've been removed from it — contact the workspace Owner.

### A page shows no data.
Check which workspace you're signed into via the sidebar switcher — dashboards, tenders, and reports show data for the active workspace only.

### Something is broken or confusing.
Use the **Support** link inside the app to report the issue (include the page and what you were doing), or reach out through the website's **Contact** page.
