A **tender** (also called a bid, RFP, or RFQ) is an opportunity where a buyer asks suppliers to submit a proposal. PMG Tracker 360 helps you record every tender you're working on, track its closing date, and manage its documents — so nothing slips through the cracks.

---

## 1. Create a new tender

1. Click **Tenders** in the left menu.
2. Click **Create Tender**.
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Tender title** — a short description of the opportunity (for example, *Supply of water meters*).
   - **Tender number** — the buyer's reference number, if they gave one (for example, `A-WS-03-2026`).
   - **Client** — choose the buyer from your [Clients Directory](/workflows/clients-directory/).
   - **Estimated value** — the bid value in rand (ZAR).
   - **Closing date** — the deadline for submitting your bid.
   - **Category** — the industry (for example, *Civil Works, IT Hardware, Consulting*).
   - **Description** — a short summary of the scope.
4. Click **Save**.

---

## 2. Tender statuses

Every tender moves through these stages:

| Status | What it means |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Open** | You're preparing the bid — documents and pricing are being put together. |
| **Under Evaluation** | The bid has been submitted and the buyer is reviewing it. |
| **Awarded** | You won! The tender is ready to become an active project. |
| **Lost / Declined** | The bid was not awarded to your company. |
| **Closed / Cancelled** | The closing date passed or the opportunity was cancelled. |

You can update a tender's status at any time by opening it and choosing the new status.

---

## 3. Documents & checklists

- **Attach documents** — upload the tender booklet, pricing schedules, and site-briefing notes to the tender so your whole team can find them.
- **Returnables checklist** — track the compliance documents the buyer requires with your submission, such as a **Tax Clearance Pin**, **B-BBEE affidavit**, **COIDA certificate**, or **CSD registration**. Tick each one off as you gather it.
- **Reminders** — set up email reminders so you get a notification **48 hours** and **24 hours** before the closing date.

---

## 4. Tender extensions & validity

- **Validity** — record how long your bid prices remain valid (in days, or as a specific date).
- **Extensions** — if the buyer extends the deadline, add an extension and attach the extension letter so the history is kept.
