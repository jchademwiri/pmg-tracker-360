---
title: Projects & Execution Tracking
description: How to convert awarded tenders into active operational projects, track delivery milestones, and monitor completion progress.
---

Once a tender transitions to **Awarded**, operational delivery begins. **PMG Tracker 360** allows you to seamlessly convert awarded bids into active projects to track execution timelines and budget allocations.

---

## 1. Initializing a Project from an Awarded Tender

1. Navigate to `/tenders` and select the awarded tender.
2. Click **Create Project from Tender**.
3. The platform pre-fills the project details automatically:
   * **Project Name**: Inherits tender title.
   * **Project Code**: Auto-generated or custom project reference.
   * **Client Link**: Mapped directly to the buyer profile.
   * **Awarded Contract Budget**: Pre-filled from the winning bid value.
4. Assign a **Project Manager** from your team and set projected start and completion dates.

---

## 2. Managing Milestones & Execution Stages

Inside any active project (`/projects/[id]`):

### Milestone Tracker
* Break down contract delivery into key milestones (e.g. *Site Survey, Phase 1 Equipment Delivery, Installation & Testing, Final Sign-off*).
* Track milestone target dates, completion percentages, and responsible engineers.

### Document Management
* Upload signed site handover certificates, delivery notes, and progress reports.
* Maintain a secure audit trail accessible to project stakeholders.

---

## 3. Project Statuses

* **Active**: Contract is under active operational execution.
* **On Hold**: Delivery suspended (e.g. awaiting client site access).
* **Completed**: All contract deliverables fulfilled, final invoices issued, and certificates signed.
* **Cancelled**: Project terminated prior to completion.
