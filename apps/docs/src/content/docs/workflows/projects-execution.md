---
title: Projects & Delivery
description: Convert a won tender into a project and track delivery, milestones, and documents — in plain language.
---

Once a tender is **Awarded**, the delivery phase begins. A **project** is where you track everything involved in delivering the contract: milestones, documents, and purchase orders.

---

## 1. Turn a won tender into a project

1. Click **Tenders** in the left menu and open the **Awarded** tender.
2. Click **Create Project from Tender**.
3. PMG Tracker 360 fills in the project for you automatically:
   - **Project name** — taken from the tender title.
   - **Client** — linked to the same buyer.
   - **Contract value** — pre-filled from the winning bid.
4. Add a **project manager** and set your **start** and **target completion** dates.
5. Click **Save**.

Your project now appears under **Projects** in the left menu.

---

## 2. Track milestones

Inside any project (open it from the **Projects** page):

- **Add milestones** — break the contract into stages (for example, *Site survey*, *Phase 1 equipment delivery*, *Installation & testing*, *Final sign-off*).
- For each milestone, set a **target date**, track its **progress** (%), and note who is responsible.

---

## 3. Documents & files

- Upload signed handover certificates, delivery notes, and progress reports to the project.
- Everything is kept together in one place with a clear history, so stakeholders can see what was submitted and when.

---

## 4. Project statuses

| Status | What it means |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Active** | The project is being delivered. |
| **On Hold** | Delivery is paused (for example, waiting for site access). |
| **Completed** | All deliverables done, final invoices issued, and sign-off received. |
| **Cancelled** | The project was ended before completion. |

---

## 5. Purchase orders from a project

Use the **Purchase Orders** section inside the project to issue orders to suppliers and sub-contractors, and watch the budget balance as orders are added. See [Purchase Orders](/workflows/purchase-orders/).
