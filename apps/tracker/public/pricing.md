# Pricing — Tender Track 360

> Note: All plans and premium upgrades are currently 100% free during the public beta phase. No credit card is required to access full features.

## Starter (Free Forever)
- **Price**: R0/month (ZAR)
- **Billing**: Free forever
- **Tender Capacity**: Up to 10 active tenders
- **PO Trackers**: 5 Purchase Orders
- **Key Features**:
  - Mandatory briefing date tracking
  - 11:00 AM closing countdown sirens
  - Email notification reminders
  - Document returnables checklist
  - Basic dashboard overview

## Professional (Standard R499/mo — Free During Beta)
- **Price**: R0 during Beta (Regular: R499/month billed monthly | R4,990/year billed annually)
- **Billing**: Monthly / Annual
- **Tender Capacity**: Unlimited active tenders
- **PO Trackers**: 50 active Purchase Orders
- **Key Features**:
  - All Starter features included
  - Multi-user team collaboration & assignments
  - Automated 90-day and 120-day validity expiry alerts
  - Line-item PO delivery milestone tracking
  - 30/60/90-day cashflow projection ledger
  - One-click Excel (.xlsx) Tender Register exports
  - Executive PDF status summaries

## Enterprise (Standard R1,499/mo — Free During Beta)
- **Price**: R0 during Beta (Regular: R1,499/month billed monthly | R14,990/year billed annually)
- **Billing**: Monthly / Annual
- **Tender Capacity**: Unlimited active tenders
- **PO Trackers**: Unlimited Purchase Orders
- **Key Features**:
  - All Professional features included
  - Multi-organization & department workspaces
  - Custom SCM ERP integration assistance
  - Role-based access control (RBAC) & audit logs
  - Priority phone, WhatsApp & email support desk
  - Dedicated onboarding specialist
